HOW TO PLAY


      
      

         
BASICS & CONTROLS

         
Use the Left / Right Arrows to move pieces side to side.

         
Use the Up Arrow to rotate Clockwise and Z to rotate Counter-Clockwise.

         
Press the Down Arrow to Soft Drop pieces faster.

         
Press Spacebar to Hard Drop a piece instantly.

         
Press C to Hold a piece for later use.

         
(Note: All controls can be changed in the KEYBINDS menu! On a touch device, use the on-screen pad.)

      


      
      

         
SCORING & COMBOS

         
Fill an entire row with blocks to clear it. Clearing multiple lines at once earns you more points and sends Garbage Lines to your opponent!

         
Chain line clears together without dropping a piece that doesn't clear a line to build a COMBO multiplier for huge bonus damage.

SPIN MECHANICS

         
When a piece is twisted into a tight spot right before locking, it performs a SPIN.
These deal extreme damage, equal to clearing double the lines!

GAME MODES

         
ENDLESS: Play forever, aiming for the highest score.

         
ULTRA: Clear 40 lines as fast as possible.

         
SURVIVAL: Survive an endless, incoming rain of garbage blocks.

         
PUZZLES: Use the exact pieces given to fully clear the board.

         
VS BOT: Battle an AI opponent. Send them garbage lines to knock them out!

         
ENDURANCE: Fight bots back to back. Each wave the bot gets harder!

         
PUYO PUYO (BETA): A different game entirely - match colored pairs to chain up your score. See the next page.

      


      
      

         
OVERTIME

         
In VS and Spectate, a match that runs long stops rewarding patience. Every clear's garbage damage is multiplied the longer the clock runs - so stalling for a perfect board eventually costs you more than it earns.

         

            
0:00 - 0:59Normal damage

            
1:00Garbage x2

            
1:30Garbage x3

            
2:00Garbage x4

         

         
You will not miss it: the timer changes colour, an indicator appears under your HOLD panel, and the edges of the screen catch fire in the same colour as the current tier - with a whoosh the instant it ignites and every time it escalates.

      


      
      

         
PUYO PUYO (BETA)

         
A different puzzle entirely from the rest of the modes - colored pairs drop onto a 6x12 board instead of tetrominoes. There is no hold, no hard drop, and no line clears here.

         
MATCH FOUR or more of the same color touching side to side or top to bottom and they pop. If puyos fall into place and complete another group right after, that is a CHAIN - each link is worth dramatically more than the last, so a slow, deliberate setup can outscore a dozen lucky pops.

         
Controls reuse your normal binds: move and rotate the same way as the other modes. There is no hard drop - holding SPACE just falls a lot faster, so you can still slide the pair into place on the way down.

         
This mode is a first pass and will keep changing - board size, drop speed and scoring are all still being tuned.
